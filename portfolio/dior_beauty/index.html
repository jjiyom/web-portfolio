    Renewing art of gifting

    Eco design packaging

    At a time when online sales are growing exponentially,
    it is of crucial importance that we strive to minimise associated negative impacts.

    As a creator of more responsible and sustainable luxury, Dior now designs
    all of its iconic gift wrapping in 100% recycled and fully recyclable paper and cardboard.
    You can also choose the lightweight shipping box available exclusively on Dior.com.
    Thanks to its 46% weight reduction, the fully recyclable minimalist packaging saves more than
    2.5 tons of cardboard and the equivalent of one ton of CO2eq for every 10,000 shipments.
